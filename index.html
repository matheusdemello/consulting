Selected work.

The starting problem, my role, and the practical value of the work.

01
Robotics · real-robot development

Picking transparent bags from a crowded bin.

Thin, transparent bags packed together are difficult to detect and grasp.

My contribution
I built a perception-to-control pipeline: separating objects in point-cloud data, estimating their position and orientation, planning grasps, and driving the robot.
Practical value
Demonstrated autonomous picking on a real robot, with pick success measured during unattended runs. The work connected visual recognition to physical action.

02
Document AI · industrial knowledge

Making engineering reports usable again.

Useful findings were scattered across Japanese reports, scanned pages, diagrams, and handwritten notes.

My contribution
I developed document extraction and retrieval that combines text, visual content, product identifiers, and technical context.
Practical value
Engineers can recover prior findings and trace answers back to the original sources, reducing the need to review reports one by one.

03
Geospatial AI · solar assessment

Assessing rooftop solar potential from aerial data.

Solar assessment needed roof geometry and local conditions, not imagery alone.

My contribution
I fused satellite imagery, LiDAR, meshes, and point clouds, then automated data preparation and model inference.
Practical value
Building-level solar-potential and investment-return assessments that account for roof slope, shading, and reflectance, with lower analysis costs.

04
Medical imaging · research & development

Developing image analysis for knee planning.

Small anatomical targets, imbalanced training data, and ambiguous boundaries make medical-image analysis difficult.

My contribution
I developed machine-learning components for bone, cartilage, and osteophyte analysis in knee-planning software.
R&D contribution
Image-analysis components for a planning workflow, with model development focused on the small structures and ambiguous boundaries that make the task difficult.

05
Scientific computing · materials research

Connecting molecular interactions to material behavior.

Understanding how molecular interactions produce collective order and material phase changes.

My contribution
I combined quantum-chemical calculations with molecular-dynamics simulations of polar liquid crystals.
Practical value
Clarified mechanisms behind orientational order, phase transitions, and domain formation, contributing to peer-reviewed research.

HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

A clear question.

Each step ends with something you can read, run, or make a decision with.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Agree on the question.

    Problem statement + success criteria
  2. 02

    Prove

    Feasibility findings + recommendation
